Kalendarik

Official holidays for your town, in the macOS menu bar
SupportPrivacy Policy

Kalendarik is a compact menu bar calendar that knows which days are official public holidays where you live — national, regional and local. In Andalusia it covers all 790 municipalities using official data from the Junta de Andalucía; in France it uses calendrier.api.gouv.fr; 30+ more countries are covered through OpenHolidays.

Moved holidays are explained, sunrise and sunset are included, and everything works offline. No account, no tracking.
